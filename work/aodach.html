Generative · Arbitrum · 69 piecesGénératif · Arbitrum · 69 pièces

Aodach was made after the artist's grandmother passed away. It is a generative collection rooted in Celtic heritage, and it treats digital patterns as a form of memory, symbolism and continuity. Each piece is an algorithmic reading of ancestral motifs, and together they turn code into a quiet tribute to lineage and loss. The collection has 69 pieces, minted on Arbitrum, an Ethereum layer 2, and it is collected through Art Blocks.
