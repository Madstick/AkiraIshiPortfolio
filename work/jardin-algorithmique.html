Jardin Algorithmique is a collection of 200 curated pieces that sit between nature and code. Every piece is built from the mathematical symmetry groups p6m and p31m, the same ones found in honeycombs and snowflakes, and the algorithm turns that strict order into compositions that look grown rather than drawn. Nothing is painted by hand: the patterns come from the meeting of natural symmetry and computational precision, and each one is different. The 200 pieces were chosen from the algorithm's outputs and minted on Base, an Ethereum layer 2. A dedicated gallery lets you browse all of them.Jardin Algorithmique est une collection de 200 pièces choisies, entre nature et code. Les 200 pièces ont été choisies parmi les sorties de l'algorithme et mintées sur Base, une couche 2 d'Ethereum. Une galerie dédiée permet de toutes les parcourir.
